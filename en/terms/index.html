1. Parties, identity and scope

PsychCon supports the private recognition and association certification system of Towarzystwo Doradców Psychologicznych (The Psychology Counselling Association), a Polish non-governmental association entered in the National Court Register under KRS 0000819107, NIP 6121869763, REGON 385092831, at ul. Warszawska 1/3, 59-700 Bolesławiec, Poland (the Association or TDP). Contact: office@psychcon.org.

The separate provider and online seller of paid platform, information technology and administrative services is Aleksandra Górczyńska-Kołodziej, an Australian sole trader, ABN 33 257 573 623, 26 River Street, Mackay QLD 4740, Australia (the Operator).

The Association issues its internal titles, appoints the assessing committee and makes substantive decisions. The Operator provides SaaS, user accounts, secure document intake and formal document checks, technical infrastructure, digital document generation and access, and transaction handling. Operating the platform does not transfer the certification decision to the Operator.

02

2. Definitions and governing documents

User means a person accessing the site; Applicant means a person applying for assessment; Member means a person with an organisational membership or status defined under the Association's rules; Committee means persons authorised by the Association to review evidence; Platform means app.psychcon.org and associated digital functions; Assessment means the separate submission and portfolio evaluation service; SaaS Service means time-limited access to specified digital features, profiles and documents.

The eligibility criteria, internal titles, documentary evidence, appeals and code of ethics are governed by the Association's current applicable rules. These Terms govern access, platform use and sales, without replacing the Association's internal instruments.

03

3. Nature of titles and decisions

TDP titles and certificates are internal, private association distinctions based on an assessment of documented achievements. They are not state licences, statutory practice rights, academic degrees or permits to practise a regulated profession.

Each holder is responsible for using the description accurately, advertising professional credentials lawfully and meeting any regulatory requirements where they practise. Recognition by employers, education providers, insurers and public authorities is not guaranteed.

04

4. Accounts and communications

Users must submit accurate account information and have access to their email. Sign-in uses a one-time code delivered by email. Accounts are personal; sharing login codes, impersonation, bulk extraction and circumvention of safeguards are prohibited.

Case updates, missing-document requests, decisions, complaints and account notices are sent through the Platform or the designated email address. Transactional messages do not constitute marketing consent. Minors should not enter paid contracts independently without any consent legally required from their parent or guardian.

05

5. Application process and document recognition

An Applicant selects a procedure and orders the assessment where a fee applies. After payment confirmation or a valid fee waiver, the Applicant may complete the form and upload evidence in the secure account. Sending the completed application to the Committee requires a separate user action.

The system may identify file types, arrange records, check file formats, completeness and version history. Automated tools may assist formal checks, but they do not decide whether a title is granted. The Committee reviews qualifications, experience, achievements and evidence individually and publishes a reasoned decision. It may seek clarification or refuse certification.

Applicants must provide authentic documents and have the right to submit them. They must not upload third-party client or patient information without a proper legal basis. Access to case records is limited by role and legitimate need.

06

6. Prices, Australian seller and Stripe

Before payment, the Platform presents the service, actual seller, total price, currency, transaction taxes, access period and any renewal. Online sales of the Operator's services are conducted by the Australian sole trader identified above. All prices in the published price list are final, inclusive of applicable taxes; no additional tax is added at checkout.

Payments are handled using Stripe. Stripe Payments may act as a payment processor without automatically becoming the seller. If a specific product is offered through Stripe Managed Payments or another Merchant of Record arrangement, the actual seller and applicable third-party terms are shown before payment; the checkout for that transaction determines the seller.

Card providers may charge their own currency conversion fees. A valid administrator-issued fee waiver is not a payment and does not guarantee an award.

07

7. Assessment fee is not a purchase of a title

The one-off assessment fee covers processing the application, examining evidence and administering the decision process; it does not purchase a title or certificate. An adverse decision, a request for additional evidence or a lower-than-expected level is not by itself defective performance.

Regardless of the Committee's outcome, statutory rights remain available for defective or undelivered services, misleading statements and applicable consumer protection.

08

8. Subscription, certificate validity and renewals

Following a favourable Committee decision, users may purchase an annual subscription to PsychCon digital services, including an active profile, a current electronic certificate and a document authenticity verification system.

The title is granted by a decision of the Psychology Counselling Association's Committee, independently of any subscription purchase. Ending a subscription does not invalidate the historical decision to grant the title, but may result in expiry of the current certificate, active status and access to selected platform features, including the ability to download a current PDF certificate showing its current validity date.

The subscription price, duration and included services are presented before purchase.

A subscription renews automatically only if the user was clearly informed of this and accepted the renewal terms when purchasing.

Users may cancel their next subscription renewal at any time through the payment portal or by contacting office@psychcon.org. Cancellation does not automatically terminate access before the end of the paid period.

After a subscription ends, earlier Committee decisions and the history of awarded titles are retained in accordance with Association rules. Reactivating digital services may require purchasing another subscription period.

The subscription fee is charged for digital services provided by the platform Operator; it is not a fee for granting a title or for membership of the Association.

09

9. Decisions, appeals and revisions

The Committee is responsible for the substantive reasons for a decision. Applicants may report clerical errors, provide requested evidence or appeal through the Platform within 30 days of receiving the decision, meaning the date on which the decision is published and made available in the applicant's account. The Platform does not permit a new appeal after that deadline.

Appeal assessment carries a separate fee according to the current price list shown before payment, unless an authorised fee waiver applies. Filing or paying for an appeal does not guarantee that the decision will change. Corrections may create immutable new revisions; earlier revisions can be marked historical, superseded or revoked with an audit trail. The Operator does not independently alter the Committee's assessment.

10

10. PDFs and QR verification

Documents are provided electronically in the available language editions. A QR code or unique link may allow anyone holding it to check authenticity, level, reference, revision, issue date, current status and the Committee's published reasons.

A published opinion behind a unique code can be accessed by anyone who has the code; it should not be treated as a private note. Private scans, correspondence, client information and full case files are not disclosed by public verification. Users should consider this before sharing their link or PDF.

11. Withdrawal rights and refunds

Consumers retain mandatory rights under applicable law, including, where relevant in the EU, the general 14-day right to withdraw from a distance contract. For digital content without a physical medium, early supply may end that right only if all applicable legal conditions are met, including express prior consent and acknowledgement. Services provided over time are subject to the separate statutory rules on performance and proportionate payment.

Buying an assessment, accepting framework terms or opening an account does not itself waive withdrawal rights. The Operator offers no additional voluntary change-of-mind refund merely because the outcome is unfavourable, but this does not restrict statutory refunds, remedies or Australian Consumer Law where applicable.

Withdrawal requests can be emailed to office@psychcon.org with the transaction reference. Where an independent Merchant of Record sells a specific order, that entity may also handle refunds under its applicable terms and law.

12. Complaints and enquiries

Complaints about access, payments, documents or digital service delivery may be sent to office@psychcon.org with the order or case identifier and a description. The Association handles substantive challenges to its decisions under its internal rules; the Operator handles its digital services and transactions.

Enquiries are dealt with without undue delay and within mandatory legal timeframes. Sensitive supporting evidence should be shared through the secure Platform, not ordinary email.

The Operator maintains reasonable security and service measures. It cannot guarantee uninterrupted internet or all third-party services but remains responsible as required by law for non-performance and non-conformity. TDP is not responsible for independently delivered professional services of holders of its private titles.

Applicants retain any rights they hold in their submissions and grant only the permissions needed for review, record-keeping and the certification procedure.
