About the association

02 / 04

People first.Decisions with care.

The Psychology Counselling Association is developing an association-based approach to recognising documented professional experience and achievements.

01

An individual perspective

Reviews consider the education, practice and achievements presented by each applicant. Applications are not automatically categorised by algorithms.

02

A commission, not a machine

Authorised individuals make the final decision. The commission may request clarification and provides written reasons.

03

Recognition with responsibility

The association recognises documented achievements while respecting professional education and legal requirements governing each service.

It matters not only what you present, but how it is considered.
