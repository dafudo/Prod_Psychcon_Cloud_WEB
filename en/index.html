Psychological Counsellor TDP. Find your pathway.

Education in psychology, pedagogy, related social sciences and overseas degree programmes can be considered as part of your individual professional record. Relevant background, practice, further training and documented achievements can matter at higher levels, too. These proposed requirements must be approved before applications are accepted.

Fields considered may include social rehabilitation, sociotherapy, educational therapy, special education, counselling, sociology and philosophy, depending on the curriculum, practical experience and further training. Explore how different backgrounds and achievements are considered.
