00First steps into practice

Psychological Counsellor in Training

For psychology students who have completed at least two years of study, started practical training and engaged in volunteering or documented publication.

Eligibility criteria for consideration
  • Currently studying psychology, with at least two years completed.
  • Psychology-related placement started or completed.
  • Documented volunteering in psychology, education or psychosocial support, or an authored/co-authored publication with a DOI.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Confirmation of psychology enrolment and completed year
  • Placement or internship evidence
  • Volunteering confirmation or DOI publication

An educational-stage recognition, not confirmation of graduation or permission to practise independently as a psychologist.

01A documented foundation

Psychological Counsellor TDPLevel I

For psychology graduates and graduates of relevant pedagogy programmes with at least a bachelor-level qualification, completed placement and developing practical experience.

Eligibility criteria for consideration
  • Completed at least a bachelor's degree in psychology or a relevant education or pedagogy programme or specialisation (such as psychopedagogy, social rehabilitation, educational therapy or special education). The relevance of study and placement is assessed individually.
  • Completed placement related to the course of study.
  • Professional or practical activity started or developing.
  • Documented volunteering, internship or DOI publication.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Bachelor-level or higher psychology diploma
  • Completed placement confirmation
  • Evidence of current activity
  • Volunteering, internship or DOI publication

Including pedagogy-related qualifications is a proposal awaiting approval. A TDP title does not confer a psychology qualification or professional licence.

02Master’s education and practice

Psychological Counsellor TDPLevel II

For graduates with a master’s degree in psychology or a relevant pedagogy field, completed placement, professional activity and additional documented achievements.

Eligibility criteria for consideration
  • Completed a master's degree in psychology or a relevant pedagogy field, including psychopedagogy, social rehabilitation, educational therapy or special education. Study content and practical experience are assessed individually.
  • Completed placement associated with the qualification.
  • Professional activity in psychology started or continuing.
  • Documented volunteering, internship or DOI publication.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Master’s degree in psychology or a relevant pedagogy field
  • Placement confirmation
  • Employment or practice documentation
  • Volunteering, internship or DOI publication

The commission considers duties and documented achievements. Including pedagogy master's degrees remains a proposal subject to approval and does not confer a psychologist's professional status.

03Established work and publications

Psychological Counsellor TDPLevel III

For psychology master’s graduates with at least three years of relevant professional experience and at least five distinct DOI publications.

Eligibility criteria for consideration
  • Master’s degree in psychology.
  • At least three years of documented professional work related to psychology.
  • At least five distinct scientific or specialist publications, each with its own DOI, authored or co-authored by the applicant.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Psychology master’s diploma
  • Confirmation of at least three years of relevant work
  • Five distinct DOI publications and evidence of authorship

The commission reviews subject matter and actual contribution, not only the number of publications.

04Advanced professional and scientific record

Psychological Counsellor TDPLevel IV

The highest standard recognition level: over five years of documented psychology practice and at least five distinct DOI publications.

Eligibility criteria for consideration
  • Master’s degree or higher in psychology.
  • More than five years of documented professional experience in psychology.
  • At least five separate scientific or specialist publications with individual DOIs, authored or co-authored by the applicant.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Psychology master’s degree or higher
  • Evidence of more than five years of professional work
  • Five distinct DOI publications and evidence of authorship

The commission considers continuity, responsibilities and contribution to knowledge and practice.

Honorary distinction

A special recognition of exceptional contributions to the association, education, scientific activity, the psychology community or social initiatives.

Eligibility criteria for consideration
  • Significant contribution to the association or its statutory aims.
  • Contribution to institutions, educational projects, scientific programmes or professional collaboration.
  • Individual, reasoned decision of the competent association body.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Documented account of contributions
  • Confirmation of significant professional or social achievements
  • Materials illustrating contribution to the community

Not a Level V and not automatically subject to the numerical criteria for Levels I–IV.

H

The “Psychological Counsellor TDP” designation is a discretionary association distinction awarded following an individual review of documented experience and achievements. It does not replace education, qualifications or authorisations required by law. Holders must comply with the rules governing the services they provide, including psychological support.

Your review journey

From application to decision.

Seven clear steps. Decisions are made by people, not algorithms, and a review fee does not guarantee an award.

  1. 01

    Before you begin

    Read the assessment principles

    Understand the association designation, levels I–IV, fees and supporting documents. Specific qualification thresholds will be published once approved.

  2. 02

    Account access

    Create your account

    When the service opens, you will sign in using your email address and a one-time link. Your account will let you resume a draft and track its status.

  3. 03

    Application draft

    Describe your professional record

    Your draft will contain personal details, qualifications, professional practice and an achievements summary. You will be able to correct your information before submission.

  4. 04

    Evidence for review

    Provide supporting evidence

    Prepare diplomas, employment or placement confirmations, education records and other relevant evidence. PDF and JPEG files will be accepted; exclude client information.

  5. 05

    PLN 300 — initial review

    Submit and pay the review fee

    Once the service terms are approved and details and declarations are validated, the application will proceed to checkout. The commission receives it only after confirmed payment.

  6. 06

    Individual review

    Respond to the commission

    The commission reviews your documents individually. It may ask for clarification or a new version of a particular attachment, with communication tied to that document.

  7. 07

    Decision PL + EN

    Receive a reasoned decision

    A positive or negative result will be documented in one bilingual Polish-English PDF. Appeals and reassessments will follow the approved rules once published.

How does the commission review DOI publications?

A DOI identifies a publication. The commission verifies existence, authorship or co-authorship, subject matter and actual contribution. A DOI alone does not prove peer review or professional competence.

Corrections, appeals and reassessments

If a document requires changes, the commission identifies what needs to be corrected. An appeal challenges an existing decision; a reassessment starts a new case. Deadlines and procedures require approval.

A review fee, not a guaranteed outcome

Initial review: PLN 300. Reassessment: PLN 349. Appeal review: PLN 199. Annual renewal of active status: PLN 149. Payment never guarantees a designation.

The TDP/PCA association designation is not a statutory professional qualification or a licence to practise a regulated profession.
