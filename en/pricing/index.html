Fees and pricing

PLN

Clearfees.

Four clearly defined services, with fees in Polish złoty. Assessment fees cover consideration of evidence, not a guaranteed outcome.

How do the fees work?

You first pay a one-off PLN 300 application review fee. Following a positive decision, the annual active-status subscription is PLN 149. Reassessment and appeal are separate services.
