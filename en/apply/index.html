Psychological Counsellor TDPLevel I

For psychology graduates and graduates of relevant pedagogy programmes with at least a bachelor-level qualification, completed placement and developing practical experience.

Eligibility criteria for consideration

  • Completed at least a bachelor's degree in psychology or a relevant education or pedagogy programme or specialisation (such as psychopedagogy, social rehabilitation, educational therapy or special education). The relevance of study and placement is assessed individually.
  • Completed placement related to the course of study.
  • Professional or practical activity started or developing.
  • Documented volunteering, internship or DOI publication.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Bachelor-level or higher psychology diploma
  • Completed placement confirmation
  • Evidence of current activity
  • Volunteering, internship or DOI publication
