Level I

For psychology graduates with at least a bachelor-level qualification who have completed placement and begun to develop relevant experience.

Eligibility criteria for consideration

  • Completed university education in psychology, at least bachelor level.
  • Completed placement related to the course of study.
  • Professional or practical activity started or developing.
  • Documented volunteering, internship or DOI publication.

Examples of supporting evidence

  • Bachelor-level or higher psychology diploma
  • Completed placement confirmation
  • Evidence of current activity
  • Volunteering, internship or DOI publication

Before sending documents

Online intake requires approval of the level I–IV criteria and applicable service terms. This page lets you prepare for the process; this preview does not collect supporting documents.
